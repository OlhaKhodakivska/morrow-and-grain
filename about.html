The story behind the studio

Spaces with soul.
People at heart.

Morrow & Grain is an interior studio built on a simple belief: a home should feel personal, comfortable, and easy to live in. Our design direction brings warmth and structure together, leaving room for your own habits, memories, and favourite things.

Discover our approach

Our point of view

Less about trends.
More about you.

We start by listening. How do you spend a quiet morning? Where does everyone gather? What makes a space feel like yours? Understanding those small routines tells us how a room needs to work before we begin thinking about how it should look.

Our starting point is the life already happening in your home. The chair you always choose, the corner that never feels finished, and the objects you would never part with all become useful clues. Good design makes room for those things instead of replacing your story with someone else’s.

Those details shape the bigger picture. We combine natural textures, grounded colours, and considered proportions to create interiors with comfort and character. A clear visual direction helps different rooms feel connected without making every space look the same.

We are drawn to materials that invite touch, lighting that works at different times of day, and furniture that earns its place. The result is an approach built around thoughtful choices, with room for a home to evolve as life changes.

The things we come back to

Thoughtful choices.
Lasting comfort.

Listen first

Your routines, needs, and favourite things are the starting point. Before choosing a colour or moving a piece of furniture, we consider who uses the space, what feels missing, and what should stay. A useful design brief begins with real life, including its less photogenic details.

Make every corner count

A practical home can be a beautiful home. We consider circulation, places to put things down, comfortable seating, and storage alongside the visual decisions. Each corner should have a purpose, even if that purpose is simply to offer a quiet place to pause.

Choose with intention

Texture, proportion, and materials give a space its quiet character. We prefer choices that work well together over details that compete for attention. Keeping a much-loved piece, repeating a natural finish, or changing the quality of light can make more difference than adding more objects.

How it comes together

A clear path to your space.

Discover

We begin with your space, your inspiration, and the changes you want to make. Photos, approximate measurements, and a conversation about your priorities help establish what matters. Together, these details form a brief that balances the feeling you want with the practical needs of the room.

Define

We bring the ideas into focus through colour, materials, furniture proportions, and layout possibilities. This stage is about making connections and setting priorities. A considered direction makes it easier to recognise which decisions belong in the room and which ones can wait.

Bring it home

We look at how the individual choices work as a whole, from the placement of a reading light to the relationship between fabrics and finishes. The goal is a coherent concept and a sensible order for making changes, so the next steps feel manageable rather than overwhelming.

A home, with a little more soul

Good design begins
with how you feel.

We believe the best spaces make everyday life a little better. A favourite chair in the morning light. A table with room for everyone. Materials that feel as good as they look. These quiet details create a home you can enjoy, rather than simply admire.

A room should support what happens in it: the working days, the unhurried weekends, the friends who stay a little longer. That is why comfort, movement, and practical decisions belong in the conversation from the very beginning.

At Morrow & Grain, we bring your story into your surroundings through considered layouts, natural textures, and details with personality. We look for a common thread between the things you already love and the changes that will make your space work better.

From an idea to a feeling

A little guidance.
A beautiful difference.

Whether you’re refreshing one corner or rethinking your home, a clear direction makes each decision easier. Our approach connects the overall atmosphere with the everyday details, so the result feels considered from the layout to the last lamp.

Interior direction

A cohesive palette, a thoughtful mood board, and a visual direction that brings your ideas together. We explore the relationship between colour, material, and light, then use that foundation to guide furniture and finish choices. The aim is a room with its own character, rather than a collection of disconnected purchases.

Space planning

A beautiful layout starts with how you move through a room. We consider furniture scale, clear walkways, storage, and the balance between shared areas and quieter corners. From an awkward living room to a flexible dining space, the focus is on making the available space feel useful and comfortable.

The finishing touches

The final layers give a room warmth and personality. Lighting changes the mood, textiles soften the edges, and meaningful objects make the space your own. We bring these elements together with the pieces you want to keep, creating a balanced interior that feels collected over time.

Find your starting point

A new beginning starts here

Let’s make room
for your ideas.

Tell us about your space, your everyday rituals, and the feeling you want to come home to. What is working already? What gets in the way? A few honest observations often say more than a perfectly organised mood board.

Include the room you want to focus on, any pieces you would like to keep, and the changes that matter most. If you have a timeframe or a budget in mind, add those too: they help turn an idea into a useful starting point.

Save a copy of your brief to keep your ideas, priorities, and inspiration together. Your brief downloads as a text file to your device.

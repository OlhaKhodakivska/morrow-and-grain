A first consultation is a chance to understand your space and turn a scattered wish list into a thoughtful starting point. Instead of trying to solve everything at once, we explore what matters most, what can stay, and which changes could make the biggest difference.

A fresh pair of eyes

Sometimes the problem is not the room itself, but the way its elements relate to one another. Looking again at furniture scale, lighting, and the path through the space can reveal possibilities you had not considered. We focus on understanding the room before suggesting new things to fill it.

A shared vision

A shared vision brings your inspiration into focus. We look at the qualities behind the images you like: perhaps soft contrast, a grounded colour palette, natural timber, or a more open arrangement. Finding those connections is more useful than trying to recreate a photograph exactly.

Your next steps

A useful starting point should make the next decision easier. We identify the priorities, note any questions that need more information, and consider a sensible sequence for changes. You can then approach the room in stages, with a clearer understanding of what the overall direction is meant to achieve.

Create your project brief

Good questions

A little clarity.

Do I need to redesign my whole home?

No. A single room, a small corner, or a fresh furniture layout can be a great starting point. It often helps to begin with the space you use most or the problem you notice every day. A focused brief keeps the decisions manageable and gives the changes a clear purpose.

What if I don’t know my style?

You do not need a style label to begin. Start with the colours you enjoy, the places where you feel comfortable, and a few images that catch your attention. We look for shared qualities across those references and use them to build a direction that feels personal rather than tied to a trend.

Can I use pieces I already own?

Absolutely. Meaningful furniture and objects can anchor a new design direction. Let us know what you want to keep and why it matters to you. Working with those pieces can help shape the palette, layout, and character of the room, while making the refreshed space feel connected to your life.

What happens when I save my brief?

The form brings your name, email address, and project ideas together in a text file that you can save to your device. Keep it as a reference, add to it as your plans develop, or share it when you are ready to discuss your space. Saving a brief does not submit an enquiry or create an appointment.
